Aggregation hook updates were changed to update the members in place and keep the aggregation address when every member keeps its address, instead of redeploying every member and the aggregation. Changing only a member's owner, paused state, capacity or other mutable field no longer replaces the aggregation, so `warp apply` and `core apply` do not set a new hook, and members that did not change are not orphaned. The aggregation is still redeployed when a member cannot be updated in place, for example when the duration of a rate limited member, the max fee of a protocol fee member, the fallback of a fallback routing member or the oracle key of an IGP member changes, when a routing member loses a destination, when the members or their types do not match, when a member that is given as an address is not a member of the aggregation, when a default or merkle tree member that is kept is bound to another mailbox than the one of the module, when the fallback of a fallback routing member that is not given as an address is bound to another mailbox than the one of the module, when the fallback of a fallback routing member is given as an address other than the deployed one, or when the token has a hybrid hook and ISM. The transactions of an update are now signed by the owners of the members that change.
